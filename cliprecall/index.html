01 / THE IDEA

Less retracing.More creating.

A sentence from a page. A link for later. An image that started something. ClipRecall keeps recent copies close, so your next thought doesn’t have to wait.

  1. 01⌘+C

    Copy

    Text, links, HTML, and images. Copy as you always do.

  2. 02⌥+V

    Recall

    Press Option + V. Choose something from your recent history.

  3. 03⌘+V

    Continue

    Press Return to restore it, then Command + V to paste.

02 / YOUR MAC. YOUR MEMORY.

Some things shouldstay close.

Your clipboard content stays on your Mac. Regular history lives in memory; pinned items are encrypted locally. No account, no cloud sync, no analytics.
