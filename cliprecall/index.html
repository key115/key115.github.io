⌥

One hotkey

Option + V opens the panel where you’re working and closes the moment you’re done. Arrow keys move, Enter inserts, Esc dismisses.

⊞

Three tabs in one panel

Clipboard, Kaomoji and Symbols together — drop in face marks and symbols without leaving the keyboard.

⇥

Paste with ⌘V

Pick an item and it goes back on your clipboard — the panel closes without stealing focus, so ⌘V pastes it where you were typing. Optional Direct Paste can paste for you once enabled in System Settings.

Tune color, opacity & blur

Terminal-inspired presets, or dial in your own. This is the real panel — try the presets, slide the opacity, swap the colors.
