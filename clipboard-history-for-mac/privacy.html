Privacy Policy

Reclip is built so your clipboard stays yours. Here is exactly what the app does — and does not — with your data.

No data collection

The app collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising. There is no profile of you, because one is never created.

No network

Reclip has no network permission at all, so it cannot connect to the internet or “phone home”. Nothing you copy is ever uploaded.

Your clipboard stays on your Mac

Regular history lives only in memory and is discarded when you quit. Pinned items are saved locally in the app’s sandbox container and encrypted with AES-GCM, using a key kept in your macOS Keychain. Nothing is synced to the cloud or sent to us.

Permissions

Accessibility is optional and is used only to paste the selected item into the app you are using. Without it, the app simply puts the item back on your clipboard for a manual paste. The Option + V hotkey opens the panel and does not log your keystrokes.

You are in control

“Delete All Data” in Settings instantly removes the in-memory history, all pinned items, the encryption key in your Keychain, and the current clipboard contents.
