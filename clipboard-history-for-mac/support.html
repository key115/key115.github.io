Quick start

Copy anything as usual. Press Option + V to open the panel where you are working. Use the arrow keys to pick an item, press Enter to insert it, or Esc to close.

The panel

Three tabs share one panel: Clipboard, Kaomoji and Symbols. Press ★ to pin an item so it stays around — pinned items are kept even after you quit.

Pasting

With Accessibility permission the app pastes straight into the active app. Without it, the item goes back onto your clipboard so you can paste it yourself with ⌘V.

Make it yours

Open Settings (⚙) to switch terminal-style presets (Homebrew, Midnight, Man Page and more) and dial in opacity and blur — readable on any wallpaper.

Privacy & data

Regular history is memory-only and clears when you quit; pinned items are encrypted locally; nothing leaves your Mac. “Delete All Data” wipes everything in one step.

FAQ

Does it sync across devices?
No. Everything stays on this Mac — there is no cloud sync or account.
Does it work without Accessibility permission?
Yes. It falls back to putting the item on your clipboard for a manual ⌘V paste.
Is there a Dock icon?
No — it is a menu-bar app that stays out of your way.
What do I need to run it?
macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.
